When people ask me what first made me start considering that the church might be deeply broken and evil, I tell them the story of how you refused to help (or even let me help) a family asking for a ride to the airport standing outside GRBC. You said something about not knowing if they really needed it and needing to support themselves. We also got into an argument about how my pursuit of missions work was unwise because it might not make enough money. 

Whenever I talked to experienced missionaries about my potential roadblocks, this conversation came up. As such, they and I always anticipated you to be one of my biggest obstacles. It surprised us that you by and large expressed and worked toward monetary support, and I did and do appreciate that. The irony of all this is that, as it turns out, you were my biggest obstacle to missions, because your abhorrent unwillingness to listen to or help strangers in need while also being willing to support your family provided a clear and concrete backdrop for me to begin wrestling with the hypocritical, murderous, and genocidal monster that is modern Christianity.

You got what you claimed to want all those years ago. I hope you’re horrified.
