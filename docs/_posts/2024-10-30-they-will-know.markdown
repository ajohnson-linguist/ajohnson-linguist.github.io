To any and all of my Christian and/or generally Republican-aligned friends and family: I highly, highly recommend taking a look at [this free zine](https://theywillknow.itch.io/they-will-know-we-are-christians-by-our-love). 

It's well-organized and shorter than it looks at first glance, and it concisely presents my experience with wrestling with how theology overlaps with love, marginalized people, and American/Western culture. It documents and interacts with self-reported reasons why members of my generation have left or cut ties with the church, and these reasons often differ greatly from the reasons that have been given for previous generations. 

I often see articles presenting the previous generations' reasons as the primary causes of church departure, but people trying to address those reasons are largely solving a problem that doesn't exist anymore. Worse, many of those past solutions exacerbate the current drivers of church departure, meaning the exact actions churches are taking to minimize losses are often increasing their losses. In my experience, the reasons this zine outlines are much, much more relevant.

I didn't write it; it was shared with me.
